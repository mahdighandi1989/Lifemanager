# Validate AI endpoint inputs and responses

## Raw Idea

The AI service at app/services/ai_service.py and its route at app/routes/ai.py likely accept user prompts without proper sanitization. This could lead to prompt injection attacks, where malicious users manipulate the AI model's behavior. Additionally, there's no validation on the AI response format, which could cause parsing errors in downstream services.
