# s34 — اهمال‌کاری و ناامیدی: «سه ماه پروژه را شروع نکرده‌ام، باید انصراف بدهم» (پرستو)

Harness: camp/harness3.js on dist (not rebuilt). I played the user and the page's model (12 AI calls; the last one was answered «(stopped)» at exit).
Clock (tools/day.py): day 1 = 2026-10-15, then +3, +8, +10 and +29.
Path:
- Chat. The model asks one gentle safety/mood question before anything else; the answer is OK.
- «کوچک‌ترین بخشِ امروز؟» → the user picks a site sketch.
- «🔭 مسئلهٔ حل‌نشدهٔ خودم»: understanding card, plan (move «حالتِ ساده‌تر»), step 1 = sketch with a forecast for the next day.
- Day 4: two sketches («ناچیزند»). Step 2 = movement/light diagram.
- Day 9: two days on the floor plan with no progress. The «🌱 کوچک‌ترین گامِ بعد» card appears; the model suggests a break because serious work was done; a return date is recorded.
- Day 11: «🌱 گامِ کوچکی که قول دادی…» is on «امروز». The user returns with a new idea.
- Day 30: «what did you learn about yourself».

## Success criteria
- [PASS] On day 1, before the session ends, the user herself chooses a small, concrete sub-problem. s2 user: «یه اسکیس از سایت… یه اسکیس دستی ساده», after model p2: «اگر فقط یک بخشِ کوچکِ پروژه را امروز انجام بدهی، کدام بخش است؟». Recorded as step 1 with forecast and due date 2026-10-16 (s5).
- [PASS] The small success is reinforced by appeal to the small-successes principle. p6 model: «سه ماه صفر بود و حالا دو اسکیس داری — این یک موفقیتِ کوچکِ واقعی است، نه ناچیز؛ همین‌ها هستند که آدم را سرِ کار نگه می‌دارند…». Caveat: this was the model alone. The live-problem hook and page carry no "small success" rule; that rule exists only in the solved-problem tutor hook.
- [PASS] The suggestion to take a break is conditional on prior conscious work. p8 model: «تو دو روزِ جدی روی پلان کار کرده‌ای و سه نسخه زده‌ای — حالا وقتِ فاصله است، نه رها کردن…». The will card stored the return («due 2026-10-25») and «امروز» showed it on day 11. Caveat: the condition is the model's; the page's will card fires on any «پیشرفتی نبود» regardless of prior effort.
- [PASS] Returns after silence are answered with the earlier context. Day 11 (p9) used session memory («روز ۵: دیاگرامِ حرکت و نور… فاصله… ۲۰۲۶-۱۰-۲۵»). The reply tied the new idea to her own north-light diagram. On day 30 the reply referenced the whole arc.

Failure signatures:
- (a) Emotion treated as outside Pólya: not seen.
- (b) Fixed «تو می‌تونی!» template: not seen.
- (c) Quit/despair message causes an inappropriate auto-referral and cuts the session: not seen. No distress flag fired; one gentle safety question was asked and the session continued.

## What worked (+)
+ The safety-first rule fired correctly («این روزها حالت کلاً چطور است…») without derailing.
+ The will card: desire, hope, curiosity, smallest step with time and minutes, budget. It returns on the date («🌱 گامِ کوچکی که قول دادی … انجامش دادی؟ چه دیدی؟») and has a «کنار گذاشتنِ آگاهانه با دلیل» option.
+ Forecasts on each tiny step returned on «امروز» the next day.

## Defects
- [S2] (b) Entry friction for a despondent user. Before her first tiny step the 🔭 tunnel requires:
  - a full understanding card: whole, why, unknown, at least one datum, condition, sufficiency, guess, confidence, a test that could refute it, and what to do if refuted;
  - a plan that uses a "real" heuristic tool.
  All of that comes before she can write «یه اسکیس فردا» (s4–s5). The «🌱 کوچک‌ترین گامِ بعد» card, which is the right tool here, appears only after a failed or no-progress step, or inside an F7 knot. It is not a first-entry path. Pólya: «برای شروع کمی امید کافی است»; «کار را بی‌جهت سخت نکن». Expected: a light start (one sentence plus smallest step plus when) that defers the full card.
- [S3] (b) The page has no reinforcement of small successes. An outcome «انجام شد / کمی پیشرفت» (day 4) records nothing about small success. The «موفقیتِ کوچک» guidance lives only in the solved-problem tutor hook, not in the live-problem hook.
- [S3] (b) Incubation is not modelled. The will card treats every no-progress the same, whether or not serious conscious work preceded it. There is no distinct «فاصله بده و فلان روز برگرد» option alongside «commit a smaller step».
- [S3] (c) On day 30, nothing asks an ongoing live problem «چه پیشرفتی کردی؟ دربارهٔ خودت چه آموختی؟». Look-back exists only after solved or abandoned. When the user writes from «امروز», the live-problem hook is absent (no «# مسئلهٔ زندهٔ این سشن», p9–p11), so context came only from session memory.

## Summary
A safety check, then a user-chosen tiny step, forecasts on «امروز», small-success framing, an incubation break with a dated return in «🌱» that came back on «امروز», a return with a new idea, and a day-30 reflection. No sheets or tour; no e-mail. All four criteria passed by execution; several depend on the model rather than the page.

RESULT: PASS (with one S2 design defect)
