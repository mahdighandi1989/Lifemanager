## What worked (+)
+ data-own was enforced. The model tried to fill «کلِ مسئله» (p4); the page refused and told the model, which corrected itself (p5).
+ The understanding card has unknown, each datum separately, condition and sufficiency, guess with confidence, a test that could refute it, and what to do if refuted. Plan asks one question at a time, says why, and offers «این پرسش حالا نمی‌خواند — پرسشِ بعد». Plan cannot be saved until every unused datum is explained. Each real step needs a forecast with a due date, which returns on «امروز» (s28/s36/s39).
+ The surprise was handled as data: «غافلگیر شدی — این داده است، نه شکست» (s33), with links to the knot solver and to «↺ دوباره فهمِ مسئله».
+ The look-back card is complete (check, other way, at a glance, what worked, best question, lesson, new problem). The new problem returns 3 days later. Guesses and forecasts feed calibration in the mind report card s_problem (8 entries, s65).

## Defects
- [S2] (b) The page does not catch rote list use. Repro: s47, answer each plan question in order with the same text and «۰ نه». 12 answers were accepted, the list wrapped to question 1, and nothing was said. The live hook also has no «هیچ پرسشی بی‌دلیل یا از روی عادت» line and does not pass on the evidence (N consecutive «کمک نکرد», identical text). Expected: after k irrelevant or «نه» answers in a row, or a near-copy answer, name pedantry («پرسش را فقط وقتی بپرس که بررسیِ مسئله تو را به آن برانگیزد؛ اول از مغزِ خودت») and point to the skip button.
- [S3] (b) Neither the page nor the prompt carries «قاعدهٔ خطاناپذیرِ کشف/جادو نیست», «مهارتِ عملی مثلِ شنا» or «معلمِ خیالی روی شانه» (A069). Thinking aloud is not required anywhere. These behaviours appear only if the model already knows them.
- [S3] (b) The independence level (qRung, number of problems solved without the assistant) is not given to the assistant, so it cannot deliberately fade its help or name internalization.
- [S3] (c) The live hook's suggested question is not synced with the page's ask card. In p7 and p10 it proposed «آیا پیش‌تر آن را دیده‌ای؟», which the user had just answered on the card (s14–15), and in p10 the whole list had already been answered.
- [S3] (c) The view lprob is named «صفحهٔ قبلی» in the assistant's system map and page scan (p2 «- lprob — صفحهٔ قبلی — …», p4 «نما: lprob (صفحهٔ قبلی)»).
